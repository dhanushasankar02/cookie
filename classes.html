What You Will Learn

Our workshops are designed to take you from a cookie enthusiast to a confident decorator. Every session includes hands-on practice, personalized feedback, and all the tools you need.

  • Dough & Baking Secrets

    Learn our signature recipe for no-spread sugar cookies that taste as good as they look.

  • Royal Icing Consistencies

    Master the 3 core consistencies: stiff for details, medium for outlining, and flood for smooth bases.

  • Color Theory & Mixing

    Understand how to achieve deep, vibrant colors and prevent color bleeding.

  • Professional Tools

    Get comfortable with piping bags, scribe tools, airbrush machines, and edible markers.
